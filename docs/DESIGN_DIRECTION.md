# Editor and graph refinement

Mode: Operate and Read. The existing sage identity is retained, as confirmed by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A student follows the reason one idea leads to another. The graph starts with a bounded neighborhood and exposes the existing links in both directions.

OWN-WORLD: Sage selection, deep ink text, neutral chrome, a white working sheet on a pale frame. Existing Inter UI and Lora note typography remain. Drawings use the separately requested stock Diagram Design palette.

STORY: Choose a concept, understand the relationship, open its note, explain it, then practice recalling it. Collected, explained, and practiced remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact graph title and focus selector sit above the canvas. A narrow topic outline stays at the left. Selecting a concept opens a readable connection ledger at the right. Dense graph labels remain hidden until selected. Notes use a single writing surface with a sticky toolbar and contextual commands.

FORM: Refine the established interface. Taste Skill dealt framed-sheet and achromatic-ledger; framed-sheet fits sustained writing and the existing sage identity. The separate Impeccable concept-roll gate was skipped during refinement. No Impeccable seed reference exists, and Taste's seed is not a substitute. This is a disclosed process limitation. The signature interaction is following a named incoming or outgoing relationship and making that concept the next focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Source adaptations

Diagram Design supplies the diagram budget, editorial palette, shape grammar, orthogonal routes, and review checks. Understand Anything supplies layered exploration, directional relationship explanations, and guided paths. Its automatic inference workflow is deliberately excluded by the user's explicit manual-linking requirement. Impeccable and Taste Skill supply task-first hierarchy, quiet chrome, progressive disclosure, and responsive checks. This is a real app screen, so marketing-page motion and imagery rules do not apply.
