# Synapse 1.5.0

Pair iPad and Android with a one-use QR code. Open Windows Settings, choose Show pairing QR code, scan it inside the tablet app and approve. Tailscale is no longer needed for this pairing mode.

The PC and tablet establish an encrypted connection through the hosted relay. Notes stay in the local vault and browser storage; the relay forwards encrypted messages without persisting note contents. Both devices need internet and the PC must stay awake with Synapse open. Offline writing remains available.

Includes camera scanning and a paste-link alternative, reconnecting, chunked image/PDF transfers, revocable device access and the existing conflict-copy recovery. Existing direct connections remain compatible.

Validated with 143 automated tests, QR image decoding, browser pairing approval, and relay integration checks. Physical iPad/Android camera and stylus checks remain pending. The Windows build is unsigned.
